could not be opened directly from the build environment** (network policy). The facts below
come from search-engine summaries of those pages, so every item still needs a quick check
with the owner before the site is published.

## Inferred (medium confidence)

- **Established 1991.** The kennitala 580491-… encodes a company registration date of
  18 April 1991 (company IDs add 40 to the day). This matches the brief. Confirm with the
  owner that the workshop itself has operated since then.

## Conflicting — must be confirmed

- **Opening hours.** 1819.is: Mon–Thu 08:00–17:00, Fri 08:00–15:00, weekends closed.
  ja.is: 08:00–12:00 and 12:30–16:15 (lunch break), Friday to 15:00.
  near-place: Friday open to 17:00. The site currently shows the 1819.is hours.
- **Summer closure.** A Facebook post announced a three-week summer closure
  (13 July – 4 August, year unknown). Not shown on the site.

## Unknown — could not verify

- Logo, brand colours and typography (Facebook images were not reachable).
- Full list of services beyond "bifreiðaviðgerðir" (repairs). Diagnostics, servicing and
  brakes/suspension are shown as **drafts** and marked for confirmation.
- Staff names, team photos, reviews and ratings. None are shown.
- Whether they are a member of BGS (Bílgreinasambandið). A search result pointed to the BGS
  member list but the page could not be opened. Not mentioned on the site.
- Any manufacturer authorisation. The site says "sérhæfing" (specialisation) only and does
  not use manufacturer logos.
